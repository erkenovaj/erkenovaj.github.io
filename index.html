I build evaluations and try to break them. My work spans constructing benchmarks (RuCLEVR, AIST/Springer 2024), attacking them (selective adversarial attacks on LLM benchmarks, SMILES 2025), and turning a puzzle game into a working assessment instrument for a 100–student evals course. Alongside evals I run alignment experiments — model welfare, unlearning traces — and I teach the material: I designed a university course from scratch, lead the “Evals for AI Safety” seminars at Monoid AI Safety Hub, and facilitate AI Risk Fundamentals at Lens Academy.

Research

Evals & benchmarks

  • Yet Another Connections Benchmark

    Preprint, 2026. A dataset of procedurally generated Connections–style puzzles for evaluating chain–of–thought reasoning in LLMs; extends our Building Connections project.

  • Selective Adversarial Attacks on LLM Benchmarks

    SMILES School Projects Proceedings, 2025

    How selectively chosen perturbations of benchmark items shift model scores — and what that implies for the integrity of the numbers the field optimizes against.

  • RuCLEVR: A Russian Diagnostic Dataset for Compositional Language and Elementary Visual Reasoning

    AIST 2024 · Springer · with K. Biryukova, D. Chelnokova, M. Tikhonova

    Diagnostic VQA dataset pairing compositional language questions with elementary visual reasoning in Russian. Dataset & code.

  • Detecting Traces of Unlearning and Inoculation

    BlueDot Impact · Technical AI Safety Project Sprint, 2026

    Ongoing study of whether unlearning leaves detectable traces in model outputs, following the “Unlearning Isn’t Invisible” (ICLR 2026) line of work. Code.

Projects & builds

  • Building Connections

    1st place, Monoid LLM Steering Hackathon, 2025

    A Connections–style puzzle game for AI safety concepts, with a puzzle generator, multiplayer rooms and a results leaderboard. Built in the hackathon (provably prompt–only at first), then developed into the weekly knowledge–check instrument for Monoid’s Evals course — rated 4.5/5 by students. Written up on the LessWrong frontpage. Code · Post

  • Bailing as an Involuntary Disgust Marker in Large Language Models

    Apart Research · Digital Minds Research Sprint, 2026

    An experimental probe of refusal–like “bailing” behaviour and disgust–like markers in language models — a model–welfare question approached experimentally.

  • ITT LPE: Idempotent Test–Time Low–Probability Estimation

    Apart Research · AI Safety × Physics Grand Challenge, 2025

    Exploring idempotent GPT–style architectures as robustness–by–design: adapting to distribution shifts while lowering the probability of catastrophic outputs. Challenge

My research focuses on evaluations for AI safety. I have built diagnostic benchmarks (RuCLEVR, AIST/Springer 2024), studied how they fail under selective adversarial attacks (SMILES 2025), and turned a puzzle game into a working assessment instrument for a 100–student evals course. In parallel I run alignment experiments on model welfare and unlearning traces, and I teach regularly: I designed and delivered a university course from scratch, lead the “Evals for AI Safety” seminars at Monoid AI Safety Hub, and facilitate AI Risk Fundamentals and Compute Verification at Lens Academy.
